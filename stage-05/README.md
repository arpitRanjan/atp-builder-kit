# Stage 5: acceptance cases and a ship bar

Put here:

- `acceptance.jsonl`: your acceptance cases (start from `evals/acceptance.jsonl`, 15 examples), written before you build.
- `SHIP_BAR.md`: the ship bar as a number, why you chose it, and who agreed to it.
- `acceptance_run.txt`: the output of `python scripts/acceptance.py --bar <your bar>` (add `--url` for your deployed app).
