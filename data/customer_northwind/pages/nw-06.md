title: Tutor pay and timesheets
visibility: tutor
---
Tutors are paid for every completed session and for sessions a student cancelled late.

- Your timesheet in **Tutor pay** fills in by itself after each session.
- Check it by the 25th of each month. Select **Flag a problem** next to any wrong line.
- Pay goes out on the 1st of the next month.

The hourly rate is in your tutor contract. Missed sessions where you did not join are not paid.
