title: Reschedule or cancel a session
visibility: student
---
To reschedule or cancel a session, open **My sessions** and select the session.

- Select **Reschedule** to pick a new time with the same tutor.
- Select **Cancel session** to cancel it.

If you cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before the start, there is no charge. Later than that,
the session counts as used. If your tutor cancels, the session is always refunded to your balance.
