title: Join a video lesson
visibility: student
---
To join a video lesson, open **My sessions** and select **Join** up to 10 minutes before the start.

- Use Chrome, Edge or Safari. Allow the camera and microphone when the browser asks.
- If the video does not load, refresh the page and select **Join** again.
- If you still cannot join after 5 minutes, select **Report a problem** on the session page.

Your tutor waits 15 minutes. After that the session is marked as missed.
