title: Safeguarding reports
visibility: admin
---
If a tutor or student raises a safeguarding concern, an admin must act the same day.

1. Open **Admin**, then **Safeguarding**, and select **New report**.
2. Write what was said or seen, with the date and time.
3. Pause the tutor's bookings if the report is about a tutor.
4. Tell the safeguarding lead by phone, then by email.

Never share report details with other tutors or students.
