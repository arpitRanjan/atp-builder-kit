title: Write session notes and progress reports
visibility: tutor
---
After each session, write short session notes in **Session notes**: what you covered and what to
practise next. Students and parents can read these notes.

Every 4 weeks, write a progress report for each regular student.

1. Open **Progress reports** and select the student.
2. Rate each topic as not started, working on it, or secure.
3. Add two or three sentences of plain advice.
4. Select **Send report**.
