title: Book a tutoring session
visibility: student
---
To book a tutoring session, open the Northwind app and select **Book a session**.

1. Pick a subject, such as maths or chemistry.
2. Choose a tutor from the list, or select **Any tutor** to see the first free slot.
3. Pick a date and time. Times show in your own time zone.
4. Select **Confirm booking**. You get a confirmation email with the video link.

Sessions are 30 or 60 minutes. You can book up to 4 weeks ahead.
