title: Upload homework and read feedback
visibility: student
---
To upload homework, open the session and select **Upload homework**.

1. Choose a photo or a PDF file, up to 20 MB.
2. Add a short note for your tutor if you want.
3. Select **Send**.

Your tutor marks the homework and writes feedback within 3 days. You get an email when the feedback
is ready. Open **Homework** to read the feedback and see your marks.
