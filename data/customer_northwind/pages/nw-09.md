title: Add or remove a tutor
visibility: admin
---
To add a tutor, open **Admin**, then **Tutors**, and select **Invite tutor**. Enter their email and
subjects. They get an invite to set up their calendar.

To remove a tutor:

1. Move or cancel their booked sessions first.
2. Select the tutor and choose **Deactivate**.
3. Their last timesheet stays open until pay day.
