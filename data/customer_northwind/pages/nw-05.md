title: Set your tutor availability
visibility: tutor
---
Tutors set the hours students can book in **Tutor calendar**.

1. Select **Availability**.
2. Drag across the hours you can teach each week.
3. Add days off under **Time away**. Students cannot book those days.
4. Select **Save**.

Changes apply to new bookings only. Sessions already booked stay on your calendar until you move them.
