title: Refund a missed or cancelled session
visibility: admin
---
Only admins can issue refunds.

1. Open **Admin**, then **Payments**.
2. Search by the student email or the booking number.
3. Check the session status: tutor cancelled, technical problem, or late cancel.
4. For a tutor cancel or a technical problem, select **Refund to balance**.
5. For a late cancel, refund only with a team lead's approval, and note the reason.

Refunds to a card take 5 to 10 working days.
