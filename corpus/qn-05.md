title: Fix sync problems
visibility: public
---
If your notes are not syncing in Quillnote, try these steps in order:

- **Check your connection.** Make sure your device is online. Open a website or another app to confirm internet access.
- **Pull to refresh or tap Sync.** In Quillnote, open the notes list and run a manual sync.
- **Confirm you are signed in to the right account.** Open **Settings > Account** and check the email or username.
- **Look for sync errors.** In **Settings > Sync**, review any warning message. If shown, follow the prompt to retry.
- **Restart the app.** Close Quillnote fully, then open it again.
- **Restart your device.** This can fix temporary network or background update issues.
- **Update Quillnote.** Install the latest app version from your device's app store.
- **Check storage space.** Low storage can block downloads or uploads.
- **Avoid editing the same note on multiple devices at once.** Let one device finish syncing before making more changes elsewhere.

If sync still fails, sign out of Quillnote, sign back in, and wait a few minutes. If possible, back up important notes first.
