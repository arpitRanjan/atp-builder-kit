title: Dark mode and themes
visibility: public
---
Use **Themes** in Quillnote to change how the app looks and feels. Dark mode can make reading easier in low light and may reduce eye strain.

- Open **Quillnote**.
- Tap or click **Settings**.
- Select **Appearance** or **Themes**.
- Choose one of these options:
  - **Light** for a bright background
  - **Dark** for a darker background
  - **System** to match your device setting

If your device switches themes automatically at sunrise or sunset, choose **System** to let Quillnote follow that schedule.

You can also try color themes:

- In **Themes**, browse the available presets.
- Select a theme to preview it.
- Save your choice to apply it across your notes and menus.

If text is hard to read, try a different theme with stronger contrast. You can switch themes at any time without changing your notes.

If the theme does not update right away:

- Close and reopen the note
- Restart Quillnote
- Check whether battery saver or device settings are forcing a specific mode

Your theme choice is saved automatically.
