---
In Quillnote, team roles control who can view, edit, organize, and manage shared notes.

- **Open your team space**
  Go to the sidebar and select your team.

- **Open team settings**
  Click **Settings**, then choose **Members**.

- **Check each role**
  Quillnote uses these common roles:

  - **Owner**  
    Full access to team settings, billing, members, notes, and folders.

  - **Admin**  
    Can manage members, shared content, folders, and most team settings.

  - **Editor**  
    Can create, edit, comment on, and organize shared notes.

  - **Viewer**  
    Can open and read shared notes. Viewers cannot edit content.

- **Change a member's role**
  Find the member, click their current role, then choose a new one from the list.

- **Invite new members**
  Click **Invite member**, enter their email, then assign a role before sending the invite.

- **Remove access**
  In the member list, open the member menu and click **Remove from team**.

- **Review permissions regularly**
  Update roles when people change projects or no longer need access.

If you cannot change roles, ask an Owner or Admin for help.
