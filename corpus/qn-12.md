Use shared templates to help your team create notes in the same format.

- Open **Quillnote** and go to **Templates**.
- Select **Team Templates**.
- Click **New Template**.
- Add a clear name, such as `Meeting Notes` or `Project Brief`.
- Write the sections your team should fill in. For example:
  - Goal
  - Owner
  - Due date
  - Action items
- If needed, add placeholder text to guide people on what to enter.
- Click **Save**.

To share a template with your team:

- Open the template.
- Click **Share**.
- Choose the team or workspace that should use it.
- Set permissions:
  - **Can use** for regular team members
  - **Can edit** for template owners or leads
- Save your changes.

To use a shared template:

- Create a new note.
- Choose **From Template**.
- Pick a template from **Team Templates**.
- Fill in the sections and save the note.

Tips:

- Keep template names short and specific.
- Update templates when your process changes.
- Remove old templates so the list stays easy to use.
- Ask one person to review template changes before publishing.
