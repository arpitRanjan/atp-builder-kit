title: Tags and folders
visibility: public
---
Use **folders** to group notes by project, class, or area of life. Use **tags** to label notes across folders, like `ideas`, `urgent`, or `recipes`.

- **Create a folder**
  1. Open the sidebar.
  2. Select **New Folder**.
  3. Type a name.
  4. Press **Enter**.

- **Move a note to a folder**
  1. Open the note.
  2. Select **Move**.
  3. Choose a folder.

- **Add tags to a note**
  1. Open the note.
  2. Select **Tags**.
  3. Type a tag name.
  4. Press **Enter** to add it.
  5. Repeat for more tags.

- **Find notes by tag**
  1. Open the sidebar or search.
  2. Select a tag, or type `#tagname`.
  3. Review the filtered note list.

- **Edit or remove a tag**
  1. Open the note.
  2. Select the tag.
  3. Rename it or remove it.

**Tip:** Folders are best for broad structure. Tags are best for status, topics, and cross-project labels. You can use both together to keep Quillnote tidy and easy to search.
