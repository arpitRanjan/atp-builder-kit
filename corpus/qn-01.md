title: Create and edit notes
visibility: public
---
To create a note:

1. Open Quillnote and sign in.
2. Select **New Note**.
3. Type a title at the top.
4. Add your text in the note area.
5. Quillnote saves changes automatically while you type.

To edit a note:

1. Open the note from your note list.
2. Tap or click anywhere in the text.
3. Add new text, delete text, or fix mistakes.
4. Your updates are saved automatically.

To format a note:

1. Highlight the text you want to change.
2. Choose a formatting option such as **Bold**, **Italic**, or **Checklist**.
3. Continue editing until the note looks the way you want.

To organize your work:

1. Use **Folders** or **Tags** to group notes.
2. Rename a note by editing its title.
3. Use search to find notes by title or keyword.

Helpful tips:

- Create short titles so notes are easy to scan.
- Use checklists for tasks and reminders.
- Review your note list often to keep things organized.

If a note does not appear to update, wait a moment and refresh the app.
