To share a notebook with your team in Quillnote:

1. Open Quillnote and select the notebook you want to share.
2. Click **Share** in the top right corner.
3. In the share panel, enter the email addresses of the teammates you want to invite.
4. Choose a permission level for each person:
   - **Can view** for read-only access
   - **Can edit** to let them add and change notes
   - **Can manage** to let them invite others and change sharing settings
5. Add a short message if needed.
6. Click **Send invite**.

Your teammates will get an invitation to join the notebook. After they accept, the notebook will appear in their sidebar.

### Tips

- Use **Can view** for people who only need updates.
- Use **Can edit** for active project work.
- Give **Can manage** only to trusted team members.
- To stop sharing, open **Share** again and remove a person or change their permission.
- If someone cannot access the notebook, ask them to check they accepted the invite with the correct email address.
