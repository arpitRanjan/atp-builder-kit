id: qn-13
title: Refund a double charge
visibility: admin
---
If a customer was charged twice for the same Quillnote purchase, you can issue a refund from the Admin Console.

1. Sign in to **Admin Console** with billing permissions.
2. Open **Billing** and select **Payments**.
3. Search by the customer email, invoice number, or payment ID.
4. Confirm there are two charges for the same order date, plan, and amount.
5. Open the duplicate charge. Check the payment status is **Captured**.
6. Select **Refund payment**.
7. Choose **Full refund** for the duplicate charge only.
8. Add a short note, such as `Duplicate charge confirmed`.
9. Review the refund details, then select **Submit**.

After you submit, the payment record shows **Refund pending** or **Refunded**. Send the customer a confirmation message and include the refunded amount and payment ID.

If the original and duplicate charges were both valid, do not refund until you confirm with the customer.

## If refund is unavailable

- Make sure the charge is not already refunded.
- Check that the payment is not still **Authorizing**.
- Verify you opened the duplicate charge, not the original one.
- If needed, contact your billing manager with the payment IDs.
