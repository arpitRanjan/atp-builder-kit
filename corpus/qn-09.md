Use these steps to review plans and manage billing in Quillnote.

- Open **Settings**.
- Select **Plans and Pricing**.
- Compare the available options.

Quillnote offers three plans:

- **Free**: Basic notes, simple organization, and sync on one device.
- **Plus**: More storage, sync on multiple devices, and offline access.
- **Pro**: Everything in Plus, plus shared notebooks, version history, and priority support.

To change your plan:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Plans and Pricing**.
2. Choose the plan you want.
3. Select **Upgrade** or **Switch Plan**.
4. Review the price, billing cycle, and included features.
5. Confirm your purchase.

To manage billing:

1. Open **Settings** > **Plans and Pricing** > **Billing**.
2. Update your payment method if needed.
3. View past invoices and your next renewal date.
4. To stop future charges, select **Cancel Plan** and confirm.

After cancellation, your paid features stay active until the end of the current billing period. Then your account moves to the Free plan. If a payment fails, Quillnote will ask you to update your payment details and try again.
