---
Use search to quickly find notes by title, text, tag, or date.

- Open **Quillnote**.
- Tap or click the **Search** field at the top of the notes list.
- Type a word or phrase from your note.
- Results update as you type.

You can narrow results with simple filters:

- **title:** finds words in note titles only  
  Example: `title:meeting`
- **tag:** finds notes with a specific tag  
  Example: `tag:work`
- **date:** finds notes from a date  
  Example: `date:2026-11-14`

Tips for better results:

- Check spelling if you do not see the note.
- Try one keyword instead of a full sentence.
- Search for a tag if you use tags often.
- Remove filters to see more results.

To open a result, select the note in the list.

If search returns nothing:

1. Clear the search field.
2. Try a different keyword.
3. Make sure the note is not archived or deleted.
4. Sync your device if you use Quillnote on more than one device.

Search helps you find notes faster, especially in large notebooks.
