# Stage 7: oversight in the product

This kit has no Chapter 7 notebook. You build on the Stage 6 harness. Put here:

- `APPROVAL.md`: where a person approves `draft_issue` in your app, what they see before they say yes, and a screenshot.
- `LOG_FIELDS.md`: the fields you log per step (start from the `traces/run.jsonl` format in `06_harness`) and who reads them.
- `feedback.jsonl`: a few examples of the feedback your app records, and the review queue it feeds.
