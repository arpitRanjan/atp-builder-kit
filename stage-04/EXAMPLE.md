# Example stage 4 (offline mock model)

Made by running `notebooks/04_baseline_vs_llm.ipynb` offline, with the Stage 1 example
`probe_summary.csv` from `checkpoint/stage-01`. The numbers are not real model results. Only the
`RECOMMENDATION` cell was edited.

What a learner must still do themselves:

- Write their own riskiest assumption and pass rule before the run, and run with a real key.
- Show their own work beyond the kit (edited cells, their own cases, a new baseline or scoring), committed in their repo.
- Read each vendor's published data terms and replace every `<date you read the page>` with the real date and their own note.
- Score their own candidates with their own Stage 1 numbers.
- Submit `<your repo link>/tree/main/stage-04`.
